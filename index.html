    Common myths
    Myth

    “I’m young and healthy, so I don’t need it.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Income protection claimants are on average aged 35–45.8 Cover is also usually cheaper the younger and healthier you are, and your premium can be locked in.

    Myth

    “My employer will look after me.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Employer sick pay varies a lot and often stops well before a long recovery. Statutory Sick Pay ends after 28 weeks.4

    Myth

    “Insurers never pay out.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    UK insurers paid ~97% of new protection claims in 2024 (life 96.5%, critical illness 89.7%, income protection 80%).5 Full, honest answers on your application help a claim go smoothly.
