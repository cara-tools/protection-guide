    Conditions and definitions vary by insurer, so always check each policy. “Part payment” means a smaller percentage of the sum assured is paid.i

    Top reason for claims

    Cancer is the single most common reason people claim on critical illness cover: almost two-thirds of claims.i

    What about your children?

    Many critical illness policies include children’s cover as standard when you take out cover for yourself, often at no extra cost. If a child is diagnosed with a specified serious condition, it pays a tax-free lump sum, which can help you take time off work or cover extra costs while they get the care they need. The amount, the conditions covered and the ages included vary by insurer.i

    What is a payment period?

    Every income protection policy has a payment period. This is the longest the provider can pay out for a single claim. There are two options: full-term and short-term income protection.

    Full-term income protection

    to retirement
    The provider pays out for as long as you need it, right up until you return to work or retire, whichever comes first
    • Long-term support for serious or lasting illness
    • Broad cover for most illnesses and injuries that stop you working
    • Can be tailored with different waiting periods and inflation-linking

    Short-term income protection

    1, 2 or 5 years
    Pays out for up to 1, 2 or 5 years per claim, depending on the policy you choosei. It pays for your chosen payment period or until you return to work, whichever comes first
    • Lower premiums and easier to afford
    • Great for gaps in employer or sick pay
    • Often suits self-employed people or tradespeople on a budget
    Common myths
    Myth

    “I’m young and healthy, so I don’t need it.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Income protection claimants are on average aged 35–45.i Cover is also usually cheaper the younger and healthier you are, and your premium can be locked in.

    Myth

    “My employer will look after me.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Employer sick pay varies a lot and often stops well before a long recovery. Statutory Sick Pay ends after 28 weeks.i

    Myth

    “Insurers never pay out.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    UK insurers paid ~98% of individual protection claims in 2025 (97.9%), including 96.7% of new life insurance claims.i Full, honest answers on your application help a claim go smoothly.

    Myth

    “It’s too expensive.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Cost depends on your age, health and the cover you choose, and it can be tailored to a budget. Starting while you’re younger and healthier usually costs less.

    Myth

    “I’m covered through work.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    Cover through work, such as death in service, usually ends if you leave the job, and it may not be enough. Cover you own stays with you.

    Myth

    “I’m a stay-at-home parent, so I don’t need it.”

    Tap to flip ↻
    Reality

    If you were gone or seriously ill, your family would still need childcare, help at home and time. Cover can help pay for that.
